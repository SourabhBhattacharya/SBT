Get the app

Install this page as an app on your phone or computer: it opens full screen from its own icon, keeps your saved list, and still works offline with the last data it downloaded.

iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap Share (the square with an up arrow), then Add to Home Screen.

Computer (Chrome / Edge): click the install icon at the right of the address bar.

    You can also type operators straight into the search box: AI AND (pricing OR retail) · sustainability NOT tourism or -tourism · "family firm" for an exact phrase · fields: journal:"Management Science", title:AI, editor:Gupta, place:Hyderabad, org:IIM, publisher:Emerald, area:finance.
