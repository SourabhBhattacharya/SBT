Calculation steps (optional)

Work the model out in named steps, like rows in a spreadsheet. Steps run from top to bottom, and each can use the inputs and the steps above it.

Click a name to insert it into the formula you're editing (outlined names are calculation steps). Use names with + - * /, ^ for powers, comparisons, and min max abs sqrt exp ln log10 round floor ceil. For conditions use IF(cond, a, b), e.g. IF(Units > 20000, 0.9, 1). Constants such as a price or an order quantity are inputs set to Fixed value.

Candidate distributions

Mean, variance and std dev are those of the fitted distribution (the data's own sample values are on the left). Lower AIC, BIC and KS D indicate a closer fit; click a row to inspect it. Parameters are maximum-likelihood estimates, except: Normal uses the sample standard deviation (n − 1, as Excel’s STDEV.S and @RISK do), and scaled beta and triangular use the method of moments. KS p-values are approximate and optimistic, because the parameters were estimated from the same data.
